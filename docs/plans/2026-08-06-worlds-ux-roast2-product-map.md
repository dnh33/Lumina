## Does map-as-atlas earn keep?

### Job audit (post-pack)

| Sub-job | Map delivers? | Duplicate when closed? | Earns keep? |
|---------|---------------|------------------------|-------------|
| Enter a curated world ≤1–2 clicks | Yes — marker navigate **or** focus Enter | Door/mood closed: **no** | **Yes** — primary path |
| Shelf heat at a glance | Legend + marker counts + status dots | Legend once in hero | **Yes** — single telling |
| Neighbor kinship / warps | Focus strip + lit edges | NeighborRail in-world | **Yes on hub** — unique fold value |
| Metaphor geography (Threshold, Reading Room, …) | Landmass labels | Door cards restate metaphor when open | **Partial** — character; not required for enter |
| Tone before enter (“Uneasy, then visceral”) | **No** on map | Door list only | Door’s *only* unique job |
| Feeling → world | **No** | Mood disclosure | Weak |

**Verdict:** Map-as-atlas **earns keep as the enter + heat + kinship surface**. It does **not** earn keep as a substitute for every prior catalog — and it no longer has to. The open question is whether kinship is used enough to justify SVG complexity vs a denser linear atlas with a warps row. At current vault size, **keep the map**; treat “prove warps” as a product metric, not a redesign trigger.
