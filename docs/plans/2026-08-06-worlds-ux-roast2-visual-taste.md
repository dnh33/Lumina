## Priority issues (visual)

### P0 — Display font never reaches Worlds titles

**What:** Live H1 “Worlds” / “Horror” compute to Geist. Markup still uses `font-[var(--font-display)]` across GenrePicker, ExperienceHero, GuidedTour, TimelineScrubber, WorldsMap focus title, GenreModules. Utility `font-display` (logo, Settings, Companion header when open) paints Cabinet.

**Why it matters:** Without Cabinet on the titles that *name* the vault, packing only shortened a SaaS app wearing booth chrome (reg-ticks, grain, map). Type is the hush. Broken type is costume.

**Fix direction (`/typeset`):** Replace Worlds `font-[var(--font-display)]` with `font-display`, or fix the arbitrary Tailwind path once. Verify H1 / desk titles / door titles at weight 600–700. Keep Geist for controls/prose. Mono for counts/eras. Do not introduce Fraunces mid-flight unless the whole product re-locks `.impeccable` vs theme — dual briefs already confuse; ship Cabinet as declared in theme.

### P0 — Competing gold vs world-accent (same viewport)

**What (live Horror Guided / Self):**

## How density stays beautiful (post-pack principle)

1. **Fold green ≠ craft green.** Measure both vh *and* whether air spotlights one object.  
2. **Pack by kinship.** Tags and moods cluster like territories — not alphabet.  
3. **One fuel job per viewport.** Gold = system/active; world-accent = in-world verbs; mist = chrome.  
4. **Type does the hush.** Cabinet for what you feel, Geist for what you do, mono for what you count — *actually applied*.  
5. **Every cut needs a richer replace.** Removing ghost `40` means a stronger shelf or seeded strip, not a flatter hero void.
