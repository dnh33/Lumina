**Lumina**  
*The definitive cinematic discovery platform that unites sophisticated AI intelligence with exquisite, Forbes-level design.*

Lumina transforms how you explore movies and television by turning your personal watch history into a living, breathing understanding of your taste — then delivering recommendations, conversations, and creative experiences that feel genuinely personal.

### Your Complete Personal Archive

Lumina makes it effortless to capture everything you’ve already seen. Build or import your library of watched movies and TV shows with a single, elegant flow. Mark individual episodes for series, rate titles on your own scale, and add nuanced notes if you wish. The system instantly learns not just *what* you’ve watched, but *why* certain stories resonated — pacing, tone, themes, directorial voice, emotional weight, and more. No more repeating suggestions or losing track of long-running shows. Your history becomes the foundation for everything that follows.

At the core of this experience is a powerful local database powered by **better-sqlite3**. Every choice, rating, note, and conversation is stored securely on your device, forming your complete private cinematic memory. Through an intelligent multi-layered **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** context system, the AI can query this personal database in real time. It retrieves only the most relevant memories and patterns to inform every response, giving Lumina an almost uncanny ability to understand and remember your taste with precision.

### Conversational AI That Actually Understands You

The heart of Lumina is a deeply integrated LLM companion that lives inside the app. Talk to it naturally about anything related to your viewing life:

> “I loved the slow-burn dread of *Dune* and the sharp dialogue of *Succession*. Suggest something that blends both but feels fresh.”  
> “I’ve seen every Christopher Nolan film. What should I watch next that gives me that same intellectual thrill without feeling derivative?”  
> “Help me choose between these three options — give me pros and cons based on my taste, no spoilers.”

Because the AI has intelligent, real-time access to your full library and conversation history through the RAG system, every response is deeply tailored. It explains why it’s recommending something, anticipates follow-up questions, and adapts instantly when you push back or refine your request. It’s less like using a recommendation engine and more like having an exceptionally well-read, insightful friend who knows your taste better than you do.

### Creative LLM Features That Go Far Beyond Suggestions

Lumina’s intelligence extends into genuinely creative territory:

- **Vibe & Mood Matching** — Describe how you’re feeling (“cozy autumn mystery,” “mind-bending escapism,” “quiet emotional drama”) and receive perfectly calibrated suggestions.  
- **Thematic Journeys** — Let the AI construct multi-title arcs or “watch orders” that tell a larger story across films and series.  
- **Intelligent Comparisons & Deep Dives** — Explore how two titles connect thematically, or receive thoughtful analysis of why a particular director or era would resonate with your history.  
- **Spoiler-Safe Creative Exploration** — Discuss “what if” scenarios, alternate interpretations, or hidden connections without ever risking a spoiler.  
- **Personalized Insights** — Receive elegant reflections on why certain titles align with your taste or discover overlooked gems that feel like they were made for you.

### A User Interface of Exceptional Polish

Every interaction in Lumina feels deliberate and luxurious. The design language is cinematic yet refined — rich dark tones, subtle film-grain textures, impeccable typography, and generous use of high-fidelity artwork. Posters and key art load with buttery-smooth animations. Discovery happens through elegant, swipeable carousels and intelligently organized grids. Your library view is beautiful and highly functional, with powerful yet simple filters and search. Detail pages are rich without feeling cluttered, offering seamless access to cast, crew, metadata, and contextual AI insights.

The AI chat is always available — either as a floating, context-aware panel or full-screen experience — yet never intrusive. Micro-interactions are delightful, loading states feel premium, and the entire app responds with the fluid responsiveness expected of world-class software. It works beautifully across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

### Powered by Comprehensive, Free Metadata

Lumina pulls rich, accurate information — posters, synopses, cast & crew details, genres, release data, and more — from trusted free public APIs (primarily TMDB). This ensures broad, up-to-date coverage of both major releases and hidden gems across film and television, delivered with speed and visual beauty.
